\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
RA-GARK is a knowledge-graph-aware recommender designed for sparse review-derived knowledge graphs. The method keeps collaborative filtering and KG semantics in two separate views: a LightGCN local view models the user--item graph, while a global view represents each item by SVD-initialized latent aspect slots and selects user-relevant slots with softmax rationale masking. A local-biased fusion gate then decides how much KG information enters the final score. The gate is initialized so that the model starts close to pure LightGCN and opens the KG channel only when training signal supports it. On a filtered Amazon Books benchmark, RA-GARK improves over both the strongest KG-aware baseline and the pure LightGCN anchor, showing that sparse KG signal can become useful when it is optional rather than mandatory.
