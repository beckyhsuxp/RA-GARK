\section{Dataset and Setup}
\label{sec:exp-data}

The experiments use a filtered Amazon Books subset with $905$ users, $1{,}399$ items, and $22{,}265$ positive interactions. The review-derived item--aspect KG contains $3{,}370$ edges over $2{,}098$ unique aspects after filtering, giving only $2.4$ aspect edges per item on average. Interactions are split by user into $70/15/15$ train, validation, and test sets with seed $42$.

We compare RA-GARK with pure LightGCN and four KG-aware baselines: KGAT, KGCL, MCCLK, and KGRec. Evaluation uses full ranking, not sampled negatives. NDCG@20 is the primary metric, with HR, Recall, and MAP as secondary metrics.

\section{Main Results}
\label{sec:exp-main}

Table~\ref{tab:main-submit} reports the Top-20 result. RA-GARK achieves the best score on all four metrics. The most important comparison is against pure LightGCN: the four KG-aware baselines are below the no-KG anchor, while RA-GARK moves above it. This supports the claim that the same sparse KG can become useful when the model can control whether and how KG signal enters the score.

\section{Ablation Study}
\label{sec:exp-ablation}

The ablation study removes one design component at a time. The largest drop comes from replacing softmax rationale masking, showing that attention normalization is a load-bearing design choice in this architecture. Removing KG-SVD initialization and removing the local-biased gate also reduce performance, which confirms that the KG channel must be both well initialized and carefully admitted.

\section{Case Study and Cost}
\label{sec:exp-case}

The qualitative case study shows why KG is not merely an auxiliary feature in RA-GARK. Because the global view selects item aspects conditioned on the user, the model can expose which semantic facets contribute to a recommendation. This supports the contribution that KG-based recommendation can reveal product characteristics beyond collaborative co-occurrence.

The additional computation is small. On the reported hardware, RA-GARK trains at approximately $1.5$ seconds per epoch, comparable to LightGCN and KGRec at the same embedding dimension. The dominant cost remains LightGCN propagation; the aspect slots, rationale masking, and fusion gates add only small batch-level operations.
